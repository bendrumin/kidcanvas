KidCanvas is a private family gallery for children's artwork.

SIGNING IN
Use the demo account above (email + password). It is pre-populated with a
family ("The Demo Family"), two child profiles (Maya and Eli), and two saved
artworks, so the Gallery, Favorites, and Profile tabs all show content
immediately. You may also create a fresh account from the Sign Up screen —
no email confirmation is required.

WHAT TO TRY
• Gallery tab — browse saved artwork; tap a piece for its detail view (title,
  artist, date, the child's age at creation, description). Tap the heart to
  favorite it.
• Scan tab — "Scan Document" uses the camera (device only). On the simulator
  or without a camera, use "Choose from Photos" to pick any image, then fill
  in the title and artist and tap Save to Gallery.
• Favorites tab — artworks marked with a heart.
• Profile tab — the family, child profiles, "Add Child", Privacy Policy and
  Help links, Sign Out, and "Delete Account".

ACCOUNT DELETION (Guideline 5.1.1(v))
Profile tab → "Delete Account" at the bottom. It asks for confirmation, then
permanently deletes the account, the family, all child profiles, and every
saved artwork, including the stored image files. Please use a throwaway
account you create yourself if you want to test this, so the demo account
stays available.

CHILDREN AND PRIVACY
Children never have accounts and never sign in. A "child profile" is just a
name and an optional birth date created by the parent, used to label artwork
and calculate the child's age. Galleries are private to the family account
and enforced at the database level. The app contains no ads, no analytics,
no tracking, and does no AI processing of artwork.
