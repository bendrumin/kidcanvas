Your kid hands you a drawing and says "it's a rainbow dinosaur that eats sunshine." The drawing survives on the fridge. The sentence is gone by next week.

KidCanvas saves both. Scan the artwork, write down what they said about it, and share the two together with the people who actually want to hear it.

HOW IT WORKS
• Scan it. The built-in document scanner finds the page, crops it, and squares it up automatically. Or pick any photo from your library.
• Write down what they said. A sentence is enough, with prompts if you are not sure what to ask. Skip it and add it later; the artwork waits.
• Share it. Every new piece and its story lands in your family's feed.

THE PART OTHER APPS SKIP
Most artwork apps are a filing cabinet: scan, sort, store. KidCanvas keeps the words that made the drawing matter, and searches them. Look up "dinosaur" a year from now and find the piece by what your child said about it.

A FEED, NOT A VAULT
• Grandparents and co-parents see each new piece the moment it is saved.
• They react and leave comments, so the artwork starts a conversation instead of sitting in storage.
• Kids never need accounts. Parents create simple artist profiles, and everything stays inside your family.
• Invite by short code. There is no public feed and no strangers.

ALSO IN THE APP
• Every piece filed to its artist, dated, with your child's age worked out for you.
• Favorites, search across titles, stories, and artists, and a gallery you can browse by child or by time.
• A nudge when it has been a quiet week, and before each birthday.

NO NONSENSE
• No ads, no trackers, no analytics in the app.
• No selling your data, ever.
• No AI processing of your children's artwork.
