The fridge is full. The backpack keeps producing more. And you can't bring yourself to throw away a single crayon sunrise.

KidCanvas turns the pile into a keepsake: scan your children's artwork in seconds and build one beautiful, private family gallery you'll actually look back on.

HOW IT WORKS
• Scan — the built-in document scanner finds the page, crops it, and squares it up automatically. Or pick any photo from your library.
• File it — every piece is saved to its artist, with the date and your child's age at creation calculated automatically.
• Treasure it — add titles and the story behind each piece, heart the frame-worthy ones into Favorites, and browse the gallery by child or by time.

MADE FOR FAMILIES
• One shared gallery for the whole family.
• Kids never need accounts — parents create simple artist profiles (a name, and a birth date if you want age labels).
• Private by default: your gallery is visible only to your family, enforced at the database level.

NO NONSENSE
• No ads, no analytics, no trackers.
• No selling your data — ever.
• No AI processing of your children's artwork.

Their art grows up fast too. Keep it.
