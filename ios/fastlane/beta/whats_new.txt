BUILD 23 (1.0.2) — the seven-feature branch, for internal testing.

WORKS IN THIS BUILD:
• The home-screen widget. Long-press the home screen, tap +, search KidCanvas,
  and add it. It shows your family's newest artwork; tapping it opens that piece.
• Quote book. Profile, then Quote book. Pick an artist and a year, make the
  book, then Share or print. It produces a real PDF, one story per page.
• Then and now. In Gallery, search a word two of one artist's drawings share
  (try "rainbow"); a chip appears to line them up oldest first.
• Voice notes. Open an artwork and record what they said, then play it back.
• Plans. Settings, then See plans. The App Store has no subscriptions
  configured yet, so it should say plans are unavailable and offer Try again.
  It should NOT spin forever; that was the bug this build fixes.
• Everything from 1.0.1: scanning, stories, the family feed, comments.

KNOWINGLY NOT WORKING:
• Push notifications. The build is signed for push, but the server keys are not
  set up yet, so nothing will arrive. The switches in Settings are safe to
  toggle.

Please report anything that looks wrong, especially in the quote book PDF.
