NEW IN THIS BUILD:

• Every button and label now uses the same capitalization, and the person you
  file artwork under is called an artist everywhere (it was "child" in a few
  places and "artist" in others).
• Artwork detail: the story card no longer runs under the tab bar, so the
  reactions and comments are reachable without hunting for them.
• Settings: choose Light, Dark, or System appearance, and turn on story
  nudges (a reminder when it has been a week, and before each birthday).
• Rename or delete your family from Settings (owner only).

Worth trying:
• Open an artwork with a long story and scroll to the comments.
• Flip appearance in Settings and check every tab.
• Turn on story nudges; the app will ask for notification permission once.
