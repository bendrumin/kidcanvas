KidCanvas is a private family gallery for your children's artwork, and the
stories behind it.

Scan a drawing with the built-in document scanner (it finds the page and squares
it up), file it to the right child with their age calculated automatically, and
write down what they said about it while you still remember. Invite grandparents
and co-parents with a short code; they can browse the gallery, read the stories,
react, and leave comments. Kids never need accounts.

No ads, no trackers, and no AI processing of your children's artwork.
