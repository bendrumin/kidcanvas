KidCanvas is a private, invite-only family gallery for children's artwork. Use
the demo account above; it is pre-seeded with a family, two child profiles, and
saved artwork, so every tab shows content immediately.

Children never have accounts; a "child profile" is a name and optional birth
date the parent creates. Comments and reactions are visible only inside a
family, which is joined by invite code; there is no public feed. Parents can
delete any comment (long-press) and remove members (Profile). Account deletion
is in Profile → Delete Account.

The camera scanner requires a device; on the simulator use "Choose from Photos".
