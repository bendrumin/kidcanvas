Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 20, 2026

KidCanvas stores your family's artwork in the cloud so everyone in your family sees the same gallery. This policy explains exactly what that involves.

What we collect

  • Account information — your email address, password (stored only as a secure hash by our authentication provider), and the name you enter. Used solely to sign you in and label your contributions.
  • Family and child profiles — the family name and any child profiles you create (name, optional birth date, avatar color). Children never have accounts, never sign in, and we never knowingly collect information from children directly — profiles are created and managed entirely by parents.
  • Artwork — the images you scan or upload, plus titles, dates, and descriptions you add. These are stored in our cloud database and storage (hosted on Supabase) so your family's devices can access them.

What we don't do

  • No advertising and no cross-site tracking.
  • The iOS app measures nothing at all — it contains no analytics SDK. The website at kidcanvas.app counts aggregate page views with Vercel Analytics (cookieless) and uses Google Analytics, which sets first-party cookies and sends usage data such as pages viewed and device type to Google. Nothing about your artwork, your children or your stories is sent to either.
  • No selling, renting, or sharing of your data with anyone.
  • No AI processing of your artwork.
  • No access to your photo library beyond the specific images you choose to upload.

Who can see your gallery

The gallery itself — titles, stories, your children’s names, dates — is readable only by signed-in members of your family account. That is enforced at the database level (row-level security), not just in the app.

Artwork image files work differently, and we would rather be precise than reassuring. Each image is served from its own address containing two random identifiers. Those addresses cannot be listed or guessed, and we never publish them — but anyone you send one to can open that image without signing in. The same is true of any public share link you create, which is the point of it, and you can delete a share link at any time. We are moving image files behind expiring signed links; until that ships, treat an image address like an unlisted link.

Deleting your data

Deleting an artwork in the app removes it from the gallery. To delete your entire account and all associated data, open an issue at the contact link below and we'll complete it promptly.

Changes
