Support & FAQ

Where are the artwork photos stored?

In KidCanvas's secure cloud database, scoped to your family account — that's what lets the whole family see the same gallery. Only signed-in members of your family can browse it. See the privacy policy for details.

Do my kids need accounts?

No. Parents create child profiles (just a name, plus a birth date if you want automatic age labels). Children never sign in and have no accounts.

Can grandparents see the gallery?

Yes. Generate an invite code from the Profile tab and they can join the family, browse the gallery, read the stories, and react or comment. The open beta is at testflight.apple.com/join/7nT5CzWQ.

What does it cost?

KidCanvas is free while in beta.

Something's not working — how do I get help?

Open an issue on GitHub with your iOS version and what you were doing, and we'll take a look.

Still stuck? Reach out via GitHub issues.
