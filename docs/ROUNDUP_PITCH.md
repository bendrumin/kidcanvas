# Pitch for "best kids-artwork apps" roundups

The buying traffic for this category lands on listicle posts ("best apps to
save kids art"), not on product homepages. These sites already rank and update
their posts yearly: KinderArt (art-storage-apps), ArtShow's blog,
YummyMummyClub, siparent.com, refinedroomsllc.com. One inclusion in a ranking
post is worth more than months of on-page tweaking.

**When to send: after the App Store release is live** — reviewers won't list a
TestFlight beta. Personalize the first line per site.

The body below is plain text on purpose — one line per paragraph, no markdown —
so it can be pasted into any email client without formatting surprises.

---

```
Subject: A story-first take on the kids' artwork app (for your roundup)

Hi <name>,

Your roundup of apps for saving kids' artwork keeps coming up when parents look for help with the fridge-door problem, and it's a genuinely useful list, so I wanted to put a new one on your radar.

I built KidCanvas after realizing the part I kept losing wasn't the drawing, it was what my kid said about it. "It's a rainbow dinosaur that eats sunshine" is gone in a week; the paper survives. So in KidCanvas the story is the point: you scan the artwork (the scanner finds the page and squares it up), write down what they said in their words, and both live together, searchable by the story text.

The rest is built around sharing with the people who actually care: an invite-only family feed where grandparents can react and comment without creating an account for the kids (children never have logins, just profiles), print-ready PDF art books with the stories included, and reminder nudges before birthdays and when it's been a quiet week.

Where it sits against the apps you already list: Artkive is archiving plus paid keepsakes, Keepy does voice recordings. KidCanvas is written stories as first-class, searchable text, plus the family feed. Free for the first 50 artworks, no ads, no AI training on kids' art, ever.

It's live on the App Store and at kidcanvas.app. Happy to set you up with a full account to poke at, answer anything, or send screenshots/assets sized however you need.

Thanks for the list either way — it's how parents actually find these tools.

Ben Siegel
kidcanvas.app · support@kidcanvas.app
```
