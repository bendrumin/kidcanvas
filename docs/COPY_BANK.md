# Copy bank

Reusable, **vetted** copy salvaged from the archived marketing docs. Everything
here is true of the product as it ships today: an iOS app, free during beta,
with no AI and no paid tier.

Anything in `docs/archive/marketing/` that isn't quoted here needs editing
before use — most of it promises "AI-powered" scanning, a "100 artworks free"
tier, or a web app as the main product.

## The one line to lead with

> The artwork is just the artifact. The story is what makes it precious.

## App Store subtitle candidates (30-char limit)

| Copy | Chars |
|---|---|
| Where artwork becomes memories | 30 |

| Every scribble tells a story | 28 |

The first three commit to the storytelling positioning; the current one sells
preservation. Pick one deliberately.

## Description openers

> Your kid brings home artwork every day. It goes on the fridge. Then another.
> And another. Soon you have a pile. You feel guilty throwing any away.

> No more paper piles. No more guilt. Just every precious memory, safely stored.

> Find "that rainbow drawing from last summer" in seconds.

> Remember what your child said about this, not just that they made it.

> Share the moment, not just the picture.

> Like a photo album, but with stories. Like social media, but just for family.
> (Original read "Like Instagram, but private" — avoid naming a competitor in
> App Store copy.)

> forgot by the next week.

## Do not ship

- **A fabricated testimonial** attributed to "Sarah M., Parent" lives in
  `docs/archive/marketing/WEBSITE_COPY_INSTAGRAM_POSITIONING.md`. Never publish
  it — inventing a review is both dishonest and an App Store violation.
- **"50+ families using it"** (in the archived Reddit post) is unverified.
- Any "Free for 100 artworks" / "$4.99/mo" line — the app is free in beta and
  the real free tier, if it ever ships, is 50 artworks.
- Any "AI-powered" or "smart scanning that automatically enhances" claim. The
  scanner does edge detection via VisionKit; there is no AI in the product.
